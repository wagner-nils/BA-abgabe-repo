\subsection{Media Framing of Energy Technologies}\label{subsec:stream_framing}

Manual studies show that energy frames and their valence vary between places \parencites[p.~6]{rochyadireetz2019framing}[pp.~177, 179]{stephens2009windmedia} and that coverage need not track deployment \parencite[p.~179]{stephens2009windmedia}. \textcite[pp.~173--174]{stephens2009windmedia} code risks and benefits within each frame. The closest published predecessor, the hydrogen study by \textcite[p.~7]{ali2025hydrogenframing}, instead codes evaluation on a separate scale, as the codebook does. The three frames added in calibration recur in this coverage \parencites[pp.~6, 9]{ali2025hydrogenframing}[pp.~177--181]{stephens2009windmedia}. Each study coded at most a few thousand articles by hand.

\subsection{Computational Framing Analysis across Languages}\label{subsec:stream_computational}\label{subsec:stream_multilingual}

Computational studies can scale further, but those reviewed mostly translate inputs or use one model per language. \textcite{toetzke2022energydiscourse}, the only energy-discourse study among them, plan to translate about 40 million paragraphs into English. \textcite{jumle2024findingframes} fine-tune on machine-translated paragraphs. \textcite{guo2023openframing} train separate English and German headline classifiers without a bilingual test set. News-frame benchmarks cover six \parencite{piskorski2023multifaceted} and nine languages, the latter scored per language on original-language test sets \parencite{piskorski2023semeval}. Headline- and sentence-framing studies compare translation with cross-lingual transfer in multilingual encoders \parencite{akyurek2020multilingualframing,akter2024scalingframing}. All score frame classification.

Translation can change labels, as it did for human quality labels of arguments \parencite[p.~310]{toledoronen2020multilingual}. Designs varying only the language of fixed content find that model behaviour differs by language \parencite{xing2024crosslingualinconsistency,hasan2026englishrewrites,xuan2025mmluprox,liang2025multilingualbehaviour}. With one untranslated prompt in eleven countries, \textcite[p.~1189]{toernberg2025outperform} finds lower accuracy outside the United States. Most evidence on language-model annotation comes from English text and the United States \parencite[p.~1183]{toernberg2025outperform}. Agreement should therefore be measured on source text, per language.

\subsection{Large Language Models as Annotators}\label{subsec:stream_llm}

Across 27 replicated annotation tasks, agreement varied widely, so every application needs its own validation \parencite[p.~1472]{pangakis2025humansintheloop}. In the closest methodological analogue, exhaustive label definitions improved coding, and adding labelled examples worked best \parencite[p.~9]{lupo2024humanlevelcoding}. From the chair's extraction pipeline, this thesis adopts temperature-zero decoding, structured output, an explicit abstention instruction and a stage-by-stage attrition account \parencite{forster2026corporatesustainability}.

Several failure modes limit model coding. Thirteen models rarely beat fine-tuned classifiers, and many errors in four analysed tasks favoured the most generic label \parencite[pp.~259--260]{ziems2024css}. Open-weight models show label bias \parencite[p.~6784]{reif2024labelbias}. List-generating models tended to concentrate probability on one label per step \parencite[p.~2472]{ma2025multilabel}. Minor prompt changes can shift label distributions \parencite{atreja2025whatsinaprompt}, and configuration choices yielded incorrect conclusions for 31 to 50\% of tested hypotheses \parencite{baumann2025llmhacking}. Repeated runs disagree, less so at lower temperatures \parencite{gilardi2023chatgpt}. Hence, the configuration is fixed before evaluation and stability is measured across executions.

\subsection{Validating Automated Content Analysis}\label{subsec:stream_validation}

A validation is only as good as its human labels and sample. In simulation, less reliable labels distorted performance estimates, yet only 14 of 37 studies validating against human coding reported intercoder reliability \parencite[pp.~554, 560]{song2020validations}. For a simulated 130{,}000-article corpus, \textcite[p.~564]{song2020validations} advise over 1{,}300 validation items with intercoder reliability of at least 0.7. Small test sets detect only large differences \parencite{card2020littlepower}. Capturing rare labels requires sufficient sample size and heterogeneity \parencite[p.~106]{feuerriegel2025nlptextdata}.

Classifying each document and counting the labels can bias category proportions even when individual labels are accurate \parencite[p.~229]{hopkins2010method}, so this thesis evaluates prevalences separately from label agreement. Even at per-language areas under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC) of 0.983 to 0.994, a model's prevalences from standard estimators stayed biased \parencite[pp.~7--8]{linder2026multicalibrated}. \textcite{egami2023dsl} combine model labels with a human-coded probability sample in a design-based estimator. Such an estimator adjusts shares towards the human coding without testing it.

\subsection{Research Gap and Contribution}\label{subsec:research_gap}

The closest studies each leave a gap. \textcite{ali2025hydrogenframing} code elements by hand, and \textcite{toetzke2022energydiscourse} plan to classify energy discourse in English translation. \textcite{lupo2024humanlevelcoding} validate only Swedish model coding, and other non-English annotation studies cover one language or pair \parencite{nemkova2025annotationskills,hohenwalde2025chatgptactors}. Testing only 7--12-billion-parameter open-weight models, \textcite[p.~188]{halterman2026codebookllms} find limited zero-shot adherence to three political-science codebooks. \textcite{linder2026multicalibrated} measure prevalence bias in four languages, but for one topic label. No reviewed study validates one language-model instrument at element level, per language and on aggregate prevalences.

Codebook prompting, per-language scoring and prevalence correction are each established. For one proprietary model on German and English paragraphs as written, this thesis combines them at element level, beside the coder's own retest. Two constraints shape the evaluation protocol: a single coder, and 120 paragraphs with rare labels, fewer than \textcite[p.~564]{song2020validations} advise.
